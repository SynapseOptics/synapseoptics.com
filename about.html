About Synapse Optics

Synapse Optics builds modern optical-design software for the next generation of engineers — and the AI agents working alongside them.

Why we exist

Optical design is dominated by a small number of tools whose architecture, pricing, and licensing all date back decades. They were built before open APIs were a default expectation and long before AI agents could take a meaningful role in engineering workflows. Synapse Optics is the modern alternative: a capable optical-design suite with a CLI, programmatic API, and MCP server open from day one.

What we believe

  • Open interfaces beat closed dialogs. Every workflow exposed in the GUI is also reachable through the CLI, the API, and an MCP server. Automate it, script it, agent-drive it.
  • Affordable beats subscription. Perpetual licenses, one-time payments, two-machine activation, free educational licenses for students and academics.
  • Documentation is the product. Optical design rewards careful work; we ship the references, case studies, and worked examples to support it.

The team

Founded by optical engineers and software engineers who wanted better tools for their own work. We're a small, focused team — reach us directly at support@synapseoptics.com.

Where we're going

LensHH-LT is the first release in the LensHH family, covering ray tracing, optimization, aberration analysis, and the full glass-catalog set. LensHH-Pro is in development and adds tolerancing, multiple configurations, environmental analysis, optical coatings, and additional surface types — for production design work.
