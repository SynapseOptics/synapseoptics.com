LensHH family — Pro edition

LensHH-Pro is coming

Everything in LensHH-LT, plus the tools production optical designs need: tolerancing, multiple configurations, environmental analysis, optical coatings, and additional surface types.

Tolerancing

Sensitivity tables and Monte-Carlo runs against fabrication and assembly tolerances. Ship designs with quantified yield, not crossed fingers.

Multiple configurations

Zoom systems, switchable optics, scan paths — one file, many states, fully optimizable across configurations.

Environmental analysis

Thermal and pressure variants of every analysis. Validate the design across the temperature and altitude range it'll actually see.

Optical coatings

Design and analysis of transmission and reflection from multilayer coating stacks. Polarized ray tracing through coated surfaces.

Additional surface types

Diffractive optics, gradient-index, freeforms — beyond LensHH-LT's spherical/conic/aspheric set.

Same engine, same workflow

LensHH-Pro shares LensHH-LT's lens editor, optimizer stack, and open APIs. Designs and skills move forward unchanged.
